How much can I borrow?

For vehicle loans, you can borrow up to 100% of the on-road price of a new vehicle, and up to 90% for used vehicles, subject to your repayment capacity.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes. Most loans allow part-prepayment or foreclosure after a minimum lock-in period. Floating-rate loans usually carry no prepayment penalty, so paying early can save you a significant amount of interest.

Do you offer refinancing?

Yes. We help you refinance or take over an existing loan from another bank at a lower interest rate, reducing your EMI and your total interest outgo. Talk to us for a quick refinance quote.

When should I apply?

As soon as your KYC, income proof and bank statements are ready. For property or vehicle purchases, apply once you have finalised the deal so the loan can be sanctioned without delays.
