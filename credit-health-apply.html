Step 1 of 6

Applicant & address

Enter the person whose credit profile is being reviewed. PAN, date of birth and address are used only for identity matching, bureau authorisation and the advisory service.

Do not share net-banking passwords, debit or credit card PINs, OTPs, bureau portal credentials or full credit reports on this page. PAN is removed from the saved draft and must be re-entered when you resume.

Step 2 of 6

What do you need help with?

Choose the main service and any additional support areas. A bureau report is obtained only after your explicit consent and only through an authorised bureau process. Dhanvi Finserv does not guarantee a score increase or a loan approval.

Additional support areas

Bureau access is consent-based

We do not pull a credit report without your authorisation. The bureau and any lender decide what they will disclose and whether to approve a loan. A credit score is one factor, not a guarantee of approval.

Step 3 of 6

Your existing credit profile

Give us an accurate picture of your current loans, credit cards, EMIs, overdue amounts and settlement history. If you are not sure, choose Not sure rather than guessing.

Accounts you want us to review

Add the loans and credit cards you know about. Enter only the last four letters or digits of an account number. Never enter a full card number, CVV, PIN or net-banking password.

Not sure about every account?

That is normal. Add what you know, and tell us in the concerns box. A bureau report, once authorised, will show the complete list. We never invent account details or assume an account is closed.

Step 4 of 6

Recent credit enquiries

Lenders record an enquiry when you apply for a loan or credit card. Too many applications in a short period can affect a lender's assessment. Add only enquiries you actually remember; the bureau report will confirm the rest.

Soft and hard enquiries

A soft enquiry, such as checking your own score, normally does not affect your score. A hard enquiry from a lender may be considered by other lenders. If you see an enquiry you do not recognise, tell us and we will help you raise it with the bureau or lender through the proper process.

Step 5 of 6

Document checklist

Upload only the documents that are relevant to your request. After submitting, you will receive a private upload link. Upload clear PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP files (maximum 5 MB each).

Likely documents for your request

Never share a full credit report publicly

A credit report contains sensitive financial and identity information. Upload it only through the private link, not over WhatsApp, email or a public URL. Dhanvi Finserv stores a bureau reference and an advisory summary, not a public copy of the full report. Access is limited to authorised staff.

Step 6 of 6

Review & consent

Check your details before submitting. This creates a credit health advisory request. It does not increase your score, remove a genuine default, approve a loan or guarantee an outcome.

Bureau access is obtained only through an authorised channel after consent and the applicable compliance checks. Dhanvi Finserv is an advisory and assistance service, not a credit bureau or a lender. Never share OTPs, portal passwords or card PINs over WhatsApp or email.
