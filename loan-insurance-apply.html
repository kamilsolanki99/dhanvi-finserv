Loan Protection Insurance

Apply for insurance assistance

Share your existing loan, the cover you need and the information an authorised insurer will use for underwriting. Your progress can be saved and resumed, and documents are uploaded through a private link after submission. This is an insurance enquiry and assistance request - it is not a policy, an approval or a guarantee of coverage or claim settlement.

Step 1 of 6

Applicant & address

Enter the person whose life or loan is being covered. These details are shared with the authorised insurer only after your consent and are used for underwriting and KYC.

We do not ask for Aadhaar, net-banking credentials, OTPs or policy portal passwords on this page. If the insurer needs KYC, we will share a secure, insurer-approved process.

Step 2 of 6

Existing loan details

Tell us about the loan you want to protect. Use the latest statement so the outstanding, EMI and remaining tenure are accurate. Insurers use these figures to assess the cover.

Why we ask

The cover amount, policy tenure and premium are assessed against the loan balance and remaining tenure. The insurer may ask for a fresh loan statement or sanction letter before issuing a policy.

Do not share net-banking passwords, debit card PINs, OTPs or insurance portal credentials with anyone, including our team. A loan account reference is not a password.

Step 3 of 6

Coverage requirement

Choose the protection you are looking for and add the nominee and co-borrower details. The final product, sum assured, premium and terms are decided by the authorised insurer.

Nominee details

The nominee receives the policy benefit under the insurer's terms. A co-borrower may need to be disclosed to the insurer and may need separate consent. Adding a co-borrower here does not make them a policyholder or beneficiary.

Step 4 of 6

Health & underwriting information

Insurers assess risk from your health, occupation and income. Answer fully and accurately. The insurer may ask for medical tests, reports or additional information before deciding on a proposal.

Full disclosure matters

Non-disclosure or inaccurate information can lead to a proposal being declined, a policy being cancelled or a claim being rejected. Dhanvi Finserv does not decide approval, premium, exclusions or claim settlement - the insurer does.

Step 5 of 6

Document checklist

Upload only the documents the authorised insurer actually asks for. After submitting, you will receive a private upload link. Upload clear PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP files (maximum 5 MB each).

Likely documents for your enquiry

The insurer confirms the final list

Typically: PAN, Aadhaar or another KYC document, a photograph, address proof, income proof, bank statement, loan statement or sanction letter, medical reports if requested, the existing policy (if any) and the insurer's proposal form. Do not upload documents that are not required. We never ask for net-banking passwords, OTPs, card PINs or insurer portal credentials.

Step 6 of 6

Review & consent

Check your details before submitting. This creates an insurance assistance request; it does not issue a policy, approve a proposal or guarantee coverage. The authorised insurer decides whether to accept the risk, at what premium and on what terms.

Dhanvi Finserv is an insurance assistance and distribution service, not the insurer. Policy issuance, coverage, exclusions, premiums and claim settlement are controlled by the authorised insurer. Never share OTPs, portal passwords or payment card PINs over WhatsApp or email.

Step 1 of 6

Insurance enquiry received

Thank you. Our insurance desk will review your details and contact you using the information provided. This is not an insurance policy, an approval, a guarantee of coverage or a claim settlement. No cover begins until an authorised insurer issues a policy and the premium is paid.
