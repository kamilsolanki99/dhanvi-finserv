Understand Your Credit Report Before You Apply Again

Get a clear, documented view of your credit profile - with bureau-supported report assistance, a line-by-line review, practical improvement guidance and a realistic loan eligibility assessment.

Your credit report is the record lenders use when they assess an application. It can show closed loans as active, duplicate accounts, an outdated overdue remark, high credit-card utilisation, missed EMIs, a settlement or write-off, or a cluster of recent enquiries. Understanding what is actually reported helps you correct what can be corrected and plan around what cannot.

Dhanvi Finserv helps you request or review your report through an authorised bureau process with your explicit consent, explains what the report shows in plain language, and prepares a practical plan. We do not erase genuine defaults, promise a score increase or guarantee a loan approval. The bureau, the lender and their policies decide those outcomes.

What this service is. Dhanvi Finserv provides credit health advisory and application assistance. It is not a credit bureau, a lender or a credit repair company. Submitting the form creates an advisory request, not a score change and not a loan application with a lender. A credit report is obtained only after your consent and through an authorised bureau process. Corrections, score changes and loan decisions remain with the bureau and the lender.

Who Can Apply?

Credit health support is open to individual borrowers and applicants. The bureau and the lender confirm what they will provide or approve.

Resident Indian Adults

Applicants must be at least 18 years old and able to provide a valid PAN, identity details and address information for bureau matching. The bureau's own verification rules apply.

Anyone Planning a Loan

Useful before applying for a home loan, LAP, business loan, personal loan, vehicle loan, education loan, gold loan or working capital facility. Review the profile before a lender records a hard enquiry.

Borrowers With Reporting Concerns

Incorrect defaults, closed loans shown as active, duplicate accounts, unfamiliar enquiries, settlement or write-off remarks. We help raise the issue through the proper bureau or lender process; the outcome depends on their verification.

Applicants Who Consent to a Bureau Check

A bureau report requires your explicit consent. You will also need to share accurate loan, EMI, overdue and settlement information. Incomplete or inaccurate details can delay the review.

Eligibility for a specific bureau report, correction or loan is decided by the bureau or lender, not by Dhanvi Finserv. A credit health request does not create a right to a correction, a particular score or a loan.

How It Works

Six clear stages from advisory request to a documented analysis. Bureau and lender decisions stay with them.

1

Submit the secure application

Complete the 6-step form with your identity, advisory requirement, credit profile, accounts, enquiries and consent. Save and resume any time. The request starts at Enquiry Received.

2

Consent and identity verification

We confirm your bureau consent and check the identity details needed for matching. A report is requested only after consent and only through an authorised bureau process. We never ask for your bureau portal password.

3

Bureau report request

The request is shared with the authorised bureau. The bureau may verify your identity directly, including an OTP or security question. We record the bureau reference only after the request is actually sent.

4

Report review and analysis

We review the report line by line: accounts, EMIs, overdue amounts, settlement or write-off entries, credit utilisation, recent enquiries and personal information. The administrator records the report date and score only after the bureau supplies them.

5

Improvement plan and eligibility view

You receive a practical improvement plan and an indicative eligibility assessment. We explain what can be corrected, what must simply age, and what a lender may consider. No score increase or approval is promised.

6

Follow-up and closure

We track follow-ups and any correction raised through the bureau or lender. Timelines depend on their verification. The request is closed when the agreed analysis is shared or you ask us to close it.

Bureau response times, correction timelines and lender decisions are outside our control. Dhanvi Finserv does not guarantee a score increase, removal of a genuine default or loan approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions about credit health advisory and how this service works.

Will Dhanvi Finserv increase my credit score?

No score increase is promised or guaranteed. We help you understand the report, identify entries that may be inaccurate and follow a lawful improvement plan. Genuine defaults, late payments and settled accounts remain on the report for the period the bureau and lender rules allow. The bureau calculates the score and the lender decides whether to approve a loan.

Can you remove a genuine default or a settled remark?

No. A genuine default cannot be erased, and no one can lawfully remove it overnight. If an entry is inaccurate - for example a closed loan shown as active, a wrong amount, a duplicate account or a payment not credited - we help you raise it with the bureau or the lender through the proper dispute process. The correction depends on their verification. A settled account may be reported as settled until the lender updates it; paying the remaining difference or obtaining a No Dues Certificate may help, but the lender decides.

Does checking my credit score reduce it?

A self-check or soft enquiry normally does not affect your score. A hard enquiry made by a lender when you apply for credit may be considered by other lenders, especially if there are several in a short period. We request a report only with your consent and through an authorised bureau process, and we explain which type of enquiry applies.

Which credit bureaus do you support?

We support CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark and Equifax where we are authorised and the bureau is available for the requested service. You can state a bureau preference in the form. Availability, authorisation and the bureau's own verification decide which report can actually be obtained.

Is this a loan application or a loan approval?

No. This is a credit health advisory request. A loan eligibility assessment is indicative only and is not an approval, a sanction, a pre-approval or a promise of an amount, rate or timeline. If you decide to apply for a loan, you must complete the lender's own application and the lender will assess it independently.

How is my credit report and personal data protected?

We collect only what the service needs, store it securely and restrict access to authorised staff. Full reports are not placed on public URLs and are not written to ordinary logs; we keep the bureau reference and the advisory summary. Documents are uploaded to a private storage bucket through a secure link. We use the information for this advisory service and handle it as described in the privacy notice. You can withdraw consent as set out there.

What do the application statuses mean?

A new request starts at Enquiry Received and can move through Consent Received, Documents Pending, Bureau Request Sent, Report Received, Analysis In Progress, Analysis Shared and Closed, with On Hold or Cancelled where applicable. Our administrator records the bureau name, bureau reference, report date, score and analysis only after the authorised bureau has supplied the information and it has been verified. A status is a record of the work done, not an assurance of a score increase or a loan approval.
