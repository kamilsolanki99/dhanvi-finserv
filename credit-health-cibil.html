Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions answered regarding Credit Health & CIBIL Advisory.

Can anyone legally erase genuine past defaults overnight?

No. Anyone claiming to erase genuine past defaults in 24 hours is misleading you. However, incorrect bank entries, wrong dates, uncredited payments, and outdated statuses CAN be legally corrected through formal bureau disputes.

Why does a 'Settled' remark harm my loan chances?

A 'Settled' status indicates that the bank took a loss and waived off some part of the outstanding interest or principal. Lenders treat this as a high risk. We guide you on paying the difference to convert it into a clean 'Closed' status.

How long does it take to see score improvement?

Legitimate bureau corrections typically take 30 to 45 days after bank verification. Score improvement through planned credit utilization and disciplined repayment takes 60 to 120 days.

Does checking my CIBIL score reduce it?

No. When you or an advisor check your own credit report, it is recorded as a 'Soft Enquiry', which has zero negative impact on your score.
