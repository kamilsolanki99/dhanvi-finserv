Loan Consolidation: Merge Multiple EMIs into One

Feb 18, 2026By Kamil Solanki0 Comments

If a large part of your monthly income disappears into three or four different EMIs, a consolidation loan can simplify your finances and reduce your monthly outgo. Instead of chasing multiple due dates, you repay one affordable EMI.

A loan against property is the most common way to consolidate. You borrow against the value of your home or commercial property and use the funds to close personal loans, credit card dues and other high-interest debt.

Consolidation works best when it lowers your total interest cost. Compare the new interest rate with the weighted average of your existing debts, and check the processing fee, foreclosure charges and total repayment.

One word of caution: a longer tenure reduces your EMI but can increase the total interest you pay. Choose a tenure where the EMI is comfortable but the loan still closes within your working years.

"One EMI, one date, one plan - consolidation does not erase the debt, it makes it manageable."

- Team Dhanvi Finserv
