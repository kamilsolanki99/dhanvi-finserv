Income Tax Return Filing, Simplified

Filing your ITR does not have to be confusing. We review your income profile and documents, prepare the return, explain the computation and complete the e-filing process only after you approve it.

Salaried employees, self-employed professionals, business owners and NRIs often have different income sources, deductions and document requirements. We confirm the applicable ITR form, the tax regime comparison and the information needed before preparation begins.

Your application is completed through a secure multi-step form with save and resume, a conditional document checklist and a review step. PAN and Aadhaar details are handled carefully, and only a masked Aadhaar reference is stored.

Secure application

Private document uploads, masked Aadhaar storage and no e-filing passwords or OTPs collected on this website.

Computation review

We reconcile Form 16, 26AS, AIS/TIS and your declared income, then share the computation for your approval.

Document checklist

A checklist tailored to salary, business, house property, capital gains, other sources and foreign income.

Verified acknowledgement

Filing is confirmed only after actual portal submission and verification of the acknowledgement.

Important: submitting an application on this website starts an assistance request. It does not file a return with the Income Tax Department. Your filing status remains Application Received until our tax team prepares the return, you approve the computation, and the return is actually filed and the acknowledgement is verified. Refunds, processing and notices are controlled by the Income Tax Department.

ITR filing support for individuals, professionals and businesses with different income profiles and filing histories.

Salaried Individuals

Salary and pension income, multiple employers, Form 16, HRA, eligible deductions, TDS and refund review.

Self-Employed Professionals

Professional and consultancy income, expenses, presumptive taxation options, advance tax and financial records.

Business Owners

Proprietorship, partnership and company-director profiles with business income, depreciation, GST records and TDS.

House Property & Capital Gains

Non-resident and RNOR profiles, foreign income or assets, DTAA considerations and India-linked income.

Pending returns, belated filings, revised returns and updated returns, subject to the rules and deadlines for the year.

Have income from more than one source, such as salary and freelance work, or salary plus capital gains? The application covers multiple income heads and we confirm the correct ITR form after review.

Documents Required

The exact list depends on your income sources and the ITR form. These are the common documents to start with.

Identity & bank

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card (masked reference stored)
  • Bank statements
  • Cancelled cheque or bank proof, if a refund is expected

Salary & tax credits

  • Form 16 from each employer
  • Salary slips
  • Form 26AS
  • AIS / TIS
  • Tax payment challans, if applicable

Business & property

  • Financial statements and books of accounts
  • Business proof or registration
  • Rent receipts and property documents
  • Capital gains statements
  • GST returns, if applicable

Deductions & history

  • Investment proofs (80C, 80D, 80E, 80G, NPS)
  • Home-loan interest certificate
  • Previous ITR and acknowledgement
  • Foreign income or asset documents
  • Pending notice or intimation copies

We confirm the final checklist based on your income, deductions and applicable tax rules. Do not send e-filing passwords, OTPs or DSC PINs over WhatsApp or email.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about ITR filing with Dhanvi Finserv.

Who can use this ITR filing service?

We assist salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, freelancers, business owners, partners, company directors, HUF taxpayers and NRIs. The application covers salary, business or profession, house property, capital gains, other sources, agricultural income and foreign income. We confirm the applicable ITR form after reviewing your profile.

Does submitting the application file my return?

No. Submitting the application creates an ITR assistance request with the status Application Received. The return is prepared only after document review, and filing happens only after you approve the computation. Your status becomes Filed & Verified only when the return has actually been filed on the Income Tax e-filing portal and the acknowledgement has been verified.

Can you file a return for a previous financial year?

We can review requests for pending, belated, revised or updated returns for the financial years available in the application. Eligibility, deadlines and late-filing implications depend on the income tax rules for that year, so we confirm these before starting. Updated returns are subject to the conditions and time limits applicable to the relevant assessment year.

How is my PAN and Aadhaar data protected?

PAN is required for the return and is stored in the application record. Aadhaar is optional at the application stage; if you provide it, only the last four digits are stored as a masked reference. We never ask for an Aadhaar OTP, e-filing password or DSC PIN on this website. Documents are uploaded to a private storage bucket and are not exposed through public URLs.

Is a refund guaranteed after filing?

No. A refund depends on the taxes paid, TDS, eligible deductions and the details in your return. The Income Tax Department processes refunds after the return is verified. We help with accurate filing and refund-bank validation, but cannot guarantee a refund amount or timeline.

What are the charges for ITR filing?

The scope depends on your income sources, the number of capital-gains transactions and the type of return. Complete the secure application and we will confirm the process and fee before starting the work. No payment is collected on the application page.
