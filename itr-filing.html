Documents Required

The exact list depends on your income sources and the ITR form. These are the common documents to start with.

Salaried individuals

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Form 16 from each employer
  • Salary slips and bank statements
  • Interest certificates and investment proofs
  • Home loan interest or rent receipts, if applicable

Self-employed professionals

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Professional income and expense details
  • Bank statements and financial records
  • Advance tax and TDS details
  • Investment and loan-related proofs, if applicable

Business owners

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Books of accounts or financial statements
  • Bank statements and TDS records
  • Depreciation and expense details
  • GST returns or other records, if applicable

We confirm the final checklist based on your income, deductions and applicable tax rules before starting the filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about ITR filing with Dhanvi Finserv.

Who can use this ITR filing service?

We assist salaried individuals, self-employed professionals and business owners. If you have income from more than one source, share the details with us and we will confirm what information is needed for your return.

Can you file a return for a previous financial year?

We can review requests for pending, belated or revised returns for the financial years shown in the enquiry form. Eligibility, deadlines and late-filing implications depend on the income tax rules for that year, so we confirm these before starting.

Will you help if I have not maintained detailed records?

Yes. Tell us what you have available - salary slips, Form 16, bank statements or business records. We will explain what can be used and what additional information may be needed. Filing is subject to the documents and details you can provide.

Is a refund guaranteed after filing?

No. A refund depends on the taxes paid, TDS, eligible deductions and the details in your return. The Income Tax Department processes refunds after the return is verified. We help with accurate filing, but cannot guarantee a refund amount or timeline.

What are the charges for ITR filing?

The scope depends on your income sources and the type of return. Share your basic details through the enquiry form, and we will confirm the process and fee before starting the work.
