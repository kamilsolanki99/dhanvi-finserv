What types of properties are acceptable for LAP?

Lenders accept self-occupied residential houses, flats, approved commercial shops, offices, and selected industrial sheds with clear marketable titles.

Can I continue using or renting the property during the loan?

Yes, absolutely! You retain full ownership, possession, and rental rights of your property throughout the loan tenure.

How is the maximum eligible loan amount decided?

Eligibility is determined by two factors: the certified technical valuation of your property (up to 60-70% LTV) and your monthly net income/cash flow capacity.

Can joint property owners apply together?

Yes, all co-owners of the property must join as co-applicants on the loan application.
