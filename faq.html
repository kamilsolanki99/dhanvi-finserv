General Questions

How much can I borrow?

Loan amounts depend on the product and your profile. With our partner banks: Home Loans and LAP from ₹5 lakh up to ₹2 crore, Personal Loans from ₹50,000 up to ₹25 lakh, Business Loans up to ₹50 lakh and Vehicle Loans up to 100% of the vehicle cost. Final eligibility is decided by the lending bank based on your income, credit score and repayment capacity.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes. Most banks allow prepayment of floating-rate loans without foreclosure charges, and many allow part-prepayment for fixed-rate loans after a lock-in period. We will confirm the exact prepayment terms of your chosen bank before you apply.

Do you help with loan takeovers and refinancing?

Yes. If you are paying a high interest rate on an existing loan, we help you transfer the balance to another bank at a lower rate and reduce your EMI. Our team manages the complete takeover process with both banks for you.

When should I apply?

You can apply as soon as you have finalised your requirement - property, education admission, vehicle or business plan. Starting early gives you time to compare offers. Keep KYC, income and bank statement documents ready to speed things up.

Which documents do I need to apply?

For most loans you need PAN card, Aadhaar card, address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITR), bank statements of the last 6 months and photographs. Property or vehicle documents are needed for secured loans. We share the exact checklist for your loan type.

After You Apply

Is my information kept confidential?

Absolutely. Your documents and personal details are shared only with the lending banks and NBFCs that are evaluating your application. We follow strict confidentiality practices and never sell your data to third parties.

How fast will my loan be funded?

Once your documents are complete, sanctions from banks typically take 2 to 7 working days. Disbursal depends on the loan type - secured loans may take longer due to legal and technical checks. We follow up with the bank daily to close your file as quickly as possible.

How do you decide the best loan for me?

We look at the total cost of the loan - interest rate, processing fees and repayment terms - and match it with your income and goals. You get a clear comparison of the shortlisted offers so you can pick what suits you best.

How much should I apply for?

Borrow what you need for your goal, while keeping your EMIs comfortable - ideally within 40 to 50 percent of your monthly income. Our advisors help you balance the loan amount and tenure so repayment never strains your budget.

Fees & Charges

How long is a loan sanction valid?

A sanction letter is typically valid for 30 to 90 days depending on the lender and loan type. If your plans are delayed, inform your bank early - in most cases the sanction can be extended on request.

What are the loan eligibility requirements and conditions?

Salaried and self-employed applicants between 21 and 60 years can apply. Lenders consider your income, work experience, credit score (700+ is preferred) and existing EMIs. The final eligibility and terms are decided by the lending bank.

How do I pay my EMIs?

Your bank sets up an automatic payment mandate (NACH) on your savings account, and the EMI is debited on a fixed date every month. You can also pay via UPI, cheque or standing instruction. We help you set up the mandate at disbursal.

After Approval

Do you charge for your service?

No. You do not pay Dhanvi Finserv for applying or for our advisory. Standard charges such as processing fees, legal or valuation charges, if any, are levied directly by the lending bank and are always communicated to you upfront in writing.

How soon will the loan amount be disbursed?

After the sanction and completion of any pending formalities, disbursal usually happens within 2 to 5 working days. For home loans, funds are released as per the payment schedule of the property. We track the process with you until the money reaches you or your seller.
