Tax profile

Tell us the year, your taxpayer category and the type of return. The final ITR form is confirmed by our tax team after reviewing your income sources.

Which ITR form applies?

ITR-1 is generally for resident individuals with salary, one house property and other-source income up to the prescribed limits. ITR-2, ITR-3 and ITR-4 apply to other income profiles, capital gains, business or professional income and presumptive taxation. We confirm the correct form before preparation.

Review & consent

Check your details before submitting. This creates an ITR assistance request; it does not file a return. Filing happens only after document verification, your approval of the computation and actual submission on the Income Tax e-filing portal.

Dhanvi Finserv is an assistance service, not the Income Tax Department. Refunds, notices and processing timelines are controlled by the Department. Never share e-filing passwords, OTPs or DSC PINs over WhatsApp or email.
