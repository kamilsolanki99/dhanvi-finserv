Salaried and self-employed applicants can apply. Final eligibility is decided by the lending bank based on your income, age, credit score and other factors.

Minimum 21 years at application; loan should close before retirement age as per the lender.

Income

Income sufficient to repay the loan; property must be owned by the applicant.

Credit Rating

A CIBIL score of 700+ improves your chances; final terms as per the lending bank.

Frequently Ask Questions

If you have a question that deals with clients, customers or the public in general, there is bound to be a need for the FAQ page.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes. Most banks allow prepayment of floating-rate loans without foreclosure charges. Conditions may differ for fixed-rate loans - we will confirm the exact terms of your chosen bank before you apply.

Do you help with loan takeovers and refinancing?

Yes. If you are paying a high interest rate on your existing loan, we help you transfer the balance to another bank at a lower rate and reduce your EMI. Our team manages the complete takeover process for you.

From paperwork to disbursal, we coordinate with both banks so you do not have to run around.

When should I apply?

You can apply as soon as you have finalised your property or requirement. Keep these documents ready:

  • KYC documents - PAN card and Aadhaar card.
  • Income proof - latest salary slips (salaried) or last 2 years ITR (self-employed).
  • Bank statements of the last 6 months.
  • Property or requirement related documents.
  • There is no application fee when you apply through Dhanvi Finserv.
