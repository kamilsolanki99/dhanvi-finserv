Loan Protection Insurance — Applicant, Loan, Coverage & Underwriting
Insurance Proposal, Quotation & Policy Record
Proposal status—
Policy status—

Only record a quotation, proposal or policy after the authorised insurer confirms it and the policy document is verified. Recording a new policy requires the insurer name, policy number and policy start date, with both statuses set to Policy Issued. After issuance, keep the proposal at Policy Issued and the policy may move to Lapsed or Cancelled while preserving the issued policy details. A quotation is not a policy and no cover begins until the policy is issued and the premium is paid. Never record approval, coverage or claim settlement on the insurer's behalf.

Bureau, Report, Analysis & Follow-up Record
Report status—

Only record a bureau reference, report date, score or analysis after the authorised bureau has actually supplied the information and it has been verified. Report Received or later requires the bureau name, bureau reference and report received date. Analysis Shared or Closed also requires the analysis summary and the date it was shared. Never promise a score increase, the removal of a genuine default or loan approval. Do not paste the full credit report into these notes or into ordinary email or WhatsApp.
