What is the minimum monthly salary needed for a personal loan?

Salaried applicants should ideally earn at least ₹20,000 net monthly income. Self-employed applicants are evaluated based on annual ITR turnover.

Can I prepay my personal loan before the tenure ends?

Yes. Floating-rate personal loans carry zero foreclosure fees. Fixed-rate personal loans typically carry a small pre-closure fee of 2-4% after a 6-month lock-in period.

Does applying through Dhanvi Finserv affect my CIBIL score?

No. Our initial consultation and lender matching involve soft profile checks that do not impact your credit bureau score.

What documents are required to apply?

Just your PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, latest 3 months salary slips, and 6 months bank statement showing salary credits.
