5 Essential Financial Habits Before You Apply for a Loan

Jan 22, 2026By Kamil Solanki0 Comments

A loan application is judged long before you fill the form - lenders look at months of your financial behaviour. Building these five habits early can improve both your approval odds and the interest rate you are offered.

First, keep your credit utilisation low. Using less than 30 per cent of your available credit limit signals that you spend within your means. Second, never miss an EMI or credit card due date, because a single default can stay on your credit report for years.

Third, maintain a stable monthly surplus. Lenders prefer applicants whose fixed obligations, including the new EMI, stay below 40 to 50 per cent of income. Keep your bank statements clean and avoid large unexplained cash deposits just before applying.

Fourth, build a long relationship with your bank. Salary credits, recurring deposits and timely repayments create an internal history that works in your favour when you ask for a larger loan or a top-up.

"Good habits are the cheapest collateral you will ever own - they cost nothing and secure everything."

- Team Dhanvi Finserv

Fifth, review your credit report at least once a year and correct errors immediately. A score above 750 is your strongest bargaining chip for a lower interest rate. Speak to our advisors to know exactly where you stand before you apply.
