Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions answered regarding Loan Protection Insurance.

Is loan protection insurance mandatory?

While banks strongly recommend or require insurance to protect the mortgaged property and cover borrower liability, you are free to choose the most cost-effective policy rather than accepting an expensive in-house bank plan.

What is the difference between Loan Cover and Pure Term Insurance?

Loan Cover pays off only the remaining loan balance to the bank. A Pure Term Plan pays the full sum assured directly to your family, who can clear the loan and retain the remaining surplus.

Can insurance premiums be added to the loan amount?

Yes. Most lenders allow single-premium loan protection policies to be funded as part of the total sanctioned loan amount, adding a nominal amount to your monthly EMI.

Does property insurance cover earthquake and floods?

Yes. Standard comprehensive property insurance covers natural calamities like earthquakes, floods, storms, and fire hazards.
