Protect the Loan, the Family and the Asset Behind It

Get help comparing loan protection cover, term life insurance and property insurance from authorised insurers - with a secure application, documented underwriting and a status you can follow.

A home, business or vehicle loan stays with the family if the borrower dies, is disabled or faces a major illness. Loan protection insurance is designed to reduce that burden. Depending on the policy, the insurer may pay the lender the outstanding balance, pay a sum assured to the nominee, or cover damage to the mortgaged property.

Dhanvi Finserv helps you understand the options, prepare the underwriting information and coordinate with authorised insurers. We do not issue the policy and we do not decide the claim. The insurer sets the premium, exclusions, waiting periods and final terms, and assesses every claim under the policy wording.

What this service is. Dhanvi Finserv is an insurance assistance and distribution service, not an insurer. Submitting an application creates an enquiry and assistance request. It is not a policy, an approval, a guarantee of coverage or a promise that a claim will be paid. A quotation is not cover. No cover begins until an authorised insurer issues a policy and the premium is paid.

Who Can Apply?

Loan protection is usually linked to an existing loan. The insurer confirms the final eligibility after underwriting.

Resident Indian Borrowers

Applicants are generally aged 18 to 75 at entry, but each insurer sets its own age limits, renewal rules and residency conditions. The insurer's decision is final.

Existing Loan With a Balance

Home loans, LAP, business loans, personal loans, vehicle loans, education loans, gold loans and working capital facilities can be considered, subject to the insurer's product rules.

Verifiable Income

Salaried and self-employed applicants should be able to support the cover amount with salary slips, ITR, Form 16, bank statements or business financials requested by the insurer.

Joint Borrowers & Co-Borrowers

A co-borrower may need to be disclosed, consent to the proposal and provide KYC or medical information. Adding a co-borrower to the enquiry does not make them a policyholder or nominee.

Eligibility, sum assured, premium, medical tests and exclusions are decided by the authorised insurer. Dhanvi Finserv does not guarantee that a proposal will be accepted or that a claim will be paid.

Coverage Options We Help You Compare

The right product depends on the loan, the asset, your family's needs and the insurer's underwriting. We help you understand the difference before you apply.

Loan Protection Cover

Credit-linked life cover designed to reduce the loan liability. If the policy pays out, the insurer typically settles the outstanding balance with the lender as per the policy terms. The cover may decrease as the loan is repaid.

Term Life Cover

A pure term plan pays the sum assured to the nominee on a valid claim. The family can use it to clear the loan and meet living expenses. The payout is not automatically sent to the lender.

Property / Fire Insurance

Insurance for the mortgaged structure against fire, lightning, storm, flood, earthquake and other perils listed in the policy. Lenders commonly require it for home loans and LAP. Cover, deductibles and exclusions vary by policy.

Loan Protection + Property

A combined enquiry for life cover on the borrower and property cover on the mortgaged asset. This is often relevant for home loans and LAP, where the lender asks for both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions about loan protection insurance and how this assistance service works.

Is loan protection insurance mandatory?

It depends on the lender and the loan. Property insurance is commonly required for a mortgaged home or LAP. Loan protection or term cover may be required, recommended or optional. Check your loan agreement and sanction letter. You can compare an insurer-approved policy instead of accepting a single in-house plan, but the lender's requirement and the insurer's terms still apply.

Does submitting this application guarantee approval or coverage?

No. Submitting the form creates an insurance enquiry with the status Enquiry Received and policy status Not Issued. The authorised insurer decides whether to accept the proposal, at what premium and with what exclusions. Dhanvi Finserv does not guarantee approval, coverage, a particular premium or claim settlement. No cover begins until the insurer issues a policy and the premium is paid.

What is the difference between loan cover and term life insurance?

A loan protection policy is linked to the loan and generally pays the lender the outstanding balance, subject to the policy terms. A term life policy pays the sum assured to the nominee, who can use it to clear the loan and support the family. The right choice depends on the loan, the family's needs, the premium and the insurer's underwriting.

Can the premium be added to the loan amount?

Some lenders allow a single-premium policy to be funded from the loan, subject to the lender's policy and the insurer's product rules. Others require the premium to be paid directly. Adding the premium to the loan increases the principal and the EMI, so ask for the total cost before agreeing.

I have a pre-existing medical condition. Can I still apply?

You can apply, but disclose the condition fully. The insurer may ask for medical reports, apply a waiting period, load the premium, exclude the condition or decline the proposal. Non-disclosure or inaccurate information can lead to a declined proposal, a cancelled policy or a rejected claim. The insurer's underwriting decision is final.

When does the cover actually start?

Cover starts only when the insurer issues the policy and the premium is paid, on the policy start date and subject to the waiting periods, exclusions and conditions in the policy document. An enquiry, quotation or proposal is not cover. Read the policy schedule and wording before relying on it.

How does Dhanvi Finserv record my policy status?

New enquiries start at Enquiry Received and Not Issued. Our administrator records a quotation, proposal or policy only after the authorised insurer confirms it and the policy document is verified. The status you see reflects that record, not an assurance that a claim will be paid. Claim settlement is always decided by the insurer under the policy.
