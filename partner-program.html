Build a Referral Business With a Documented Process

Dhanvi Finserv works with DSAs, referral partners and financial-services professionals who introduce customers for loans and financial services. Onboarding is a written application with document verification, an approval decision and a signed agreement before any lead is allocated or a partner login is issued.

The partner network is for professionals who already meet customers in the normal course of their work - chartered accountants, tax consultants, property brokers, insurance advisors, financial-services companies and individual connectors. You introduce a customer's requirement, and the Dhanvi Finserv team handles the product explanation, documentation and lender coordination.

Every application is reviewed on its own merits. Approval depends on the accuracy of the information, the verification of your identity and business documents, your experience and the fit with our product range. Approval is discretionary, and an application or agreement does not create an entitlement to leads, a loan approval, a disbursal or a particular commission.

What this programme is. It is a documented DSA and referral onboarding process for eligible professionals and firms. It is not a job offer, an agency appointment by itself, a promise of lead volume or a guarantee of commission. Partner login is provisioned only after written approval and a signed agreement that has taken effect. Dhanvi Finserv does not approve loans or decide credit outcomes - the lender does.

How referrals are handled

A referral is accepted only with the customer's knowledge and consent. The customer must be told that you are introducing them to Dhanvi Finserv, and the product, fee and terms are explained to the customer by the team handling the file.

  • No referral fee may be charged to the customer unless the signed agreement permits it and the customer agrees in writing.
  • Do not collect customer OTPs, card PINs, net-banking passwords or signed blank forms.
  • Do not promise a loan approval, sanction, disbursal, interest rate, timeline or commission.
  • Share customer information only through the documented channel provided after approval.

Product names describe the category of assistance. The lender's own eligibility rules, credit policy and documentation decide whether a customer is approved and on what terms.

Onboarding Process

Five stages from application to an active partner login. Each stage is separate, and progress depends on the checks at that stage.

1

Submit the secure application

Complete the five-step form with your applicant details, firm and office address, PAN and business registration, partner type, experience, products, expected referrals and preferred locations. Save and resume any time. The application starts at Application Received.

2

Upload documents and verify

Use the private upload link to submit PAN, address proof, photograph, business registration and onboarding documents. The verification team checks PAN, business registration, address, photograph and bank / DSA associations. Missing or unclear documents delay the review.

3

Review and approval decision

An executive reviews the application, documents and verification findings. The application may move to Under Review, Documents Pending, Verification In Progress, On Hold, Approved or Rejected. Approval requires every required document and verification to be marked Verified and an executive to be assigned.

4

Agreement and login provisioning

After approval, the partner agreement is prepared with the commission terms, effective dates and reference. A partner login is provisioned only when the agreement is signed, has taken effect and the login email matches the approved application. Until then, no portal access or lead allocation is created.

5

Lead allocation and servicing

An active partner may receive leads based on customer location, product fit, partner capacity and lender requirements. Each assignment is recorded with a reference and status. The partner updates progress in the portal, and the Dhanvi Finserv team manages the lender process and commission record.

Lead allocation is not guaranteed at any stage. Approval, an agreement and an active login do not create a right to a particular volume of leads, a sanction, a disbursal or a commission.

Commission Transparency

Commission is governed by the signed partner agreement and the actual disbursal - not by a lead assignment, an application or a verbal estimate.

The agreement records the commission type, the basis and rate where applicable, the payment process and the documents required from you. A commission record is created against the related lead assignment and disbursal, and its status is visible in the partner portal.

Commission is normally payable after the lender disburses the loan and pays Dhanvi Finserv, and after the customer's file is complete. Tax is deducted at source where the law requires it, and the net payable is shown after deduction. A commission is not payable merely because a lead was assigned or an application was submitted.

If a loan is cancelled, reversed, repaid early or repurchased by the lender, the commission may be revised or recovered as set out in the agreement. Any dispute is handled under the agreement's dispute clause.

Commission status What it means
Pending Recorded, but the disbursal, lender payment or file completion is still pending.
Approved Checked against the agreement and the disbursal, and approved for payment.
Invoiced An invoice or payout statement has been raised for the approved amount.
Paid Paid to the bank account on record, with a payment reference.
On Hold Payment is paused for a documented reason such as a pending clarification or lender reconciliation.
Cancelled The commission no longer applies, for example because the loan was cancelled or reversed.
No guaranteed payout. Dhanvi Finserv does not guarantee a commission amount, a payment date or a particular commission rate outside the signed agreement. The lender decides whether to sanction and disburse the loan, and the agreement governs the commission that follows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions about eligibility, approval, login, leads and commission.

Who can join the Dhanvi Finserv partner network?

Individual referral partners, chartered accountants, tax consultants, property consultants, insurance advisors, financial-services companies and firms with an existing DSA or referral operation can apply. You need a valid PAN, an active mobile number and email, a verifiable office address and the ability to complete document verification. Meeting these criteria makes your application eligible for review; it does not guarantee approval.

Do I need a registered company to apply?

No. A proprietorship or an individual referral practice can apply, and the form allows "Not registered" as the business registration type. If you select a registered type, the registration number and the matching certificate are required, and a business registration verification is completed before approval. A registered firm may need to provide an authorisation letter for the person signing the agreement.

Does applying guarantee leads, loan approvals or commission?

No. Dhanvi Finserv does not guarantee any lead volume, loan approval, sanction, disbursal, turnaround time or commission amount. Leads are allocated based on customer location, product fit, partner capacity, verification status and lender requirements. The lender decides the credit outcome, and the signed agreement governs commission. An application, approval or login is not a promise of business volume.

What documents will I need to provide?

Usually PAN, current address proof, a recent passport-size photograph, business registration documents if your firm is registered, bank proof for the payout account and any onboarding document requested by the team. GST and incorporation documents may also be requested. Upload them through the private link generated after submission. Files must be PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP and no larger than 5 MB each.

How does approval and partner login work?

After submission, the team reviews your application, verifies the required documents and records the verification outcome. Approval requires every required document and verification to be marked Verified and an executive to be assigned. After approval, an agreement is prepared. A partner login is provisioned only when the agreement is signed, has taken effect and the login email matches the approved application. You will be told in writing when the login is ready.

How is commission calculated and paid?

The signed agreement records the commission type, the basis and rate where applicable, and the payment process. A commission is recorded against the related lead assignment and actual disbursal, and is normally payable after the lender disburses the loan and pays Dhanvi Finserv. Tax is deducted at source where required, and the net payable is shown. Commission is not payable merely because a lead was assigned or an application was submitted. If a loan is cancelled, reversed or repaid early, the commission may be revised or recovered as the agreement provides.

What customer information can I see in the partner portal?

Only the leads assigned to you and the records connected to your own partner profile. The portal is read-only apart from updating the progress of an assigned lead to Contacted, Documents Pending or Submitted to Lender. You cannot browse other partners' leads, customer documents, commissions or follow-ups. Access is enforced at the API and database level, and access may be suspended if it is misused. Never copy customer information into personal storage, messaging apps or another system.
