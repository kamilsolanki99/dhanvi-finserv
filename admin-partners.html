Partner agreement

Review decision

Approval requires every required document and verification to be Verified and an executive to be assigned. The database validates these conditions again. Rejection requires a written reason. No lead volume, loan approval, sanction, disbursal or commission is guaranteed by approval.

Partner login provisioning

Provisioning requires an Approved application, a Signed or Active agreement with a signed date and an effective-from date that has arrived, and an auth user whose email exactly matches the application email. The function grants the partner role and activates the profile. Never provision a login before the agreement is effective.

Partner profile

Profile and performance
Account status

Suspending or inactivating a profile does not delete leads, agreements or commission records. The partner portal checks the status on every sign-in. Reactivating restores access only if the auth account and agreement remain valid.

Progress and lender outcome

Sanction and disbursal are recorded only from the lender's written outcome. A partner may not set these statuses. The assignment record controls what the partner can see; never assign a customer without their knowledge and consent.

Add commission record

A commission is recorded only against an actual disbursal and the signed agreement. Do not record a commission merely because a lead was assigned or an application was submitted. TDS is deducted where the law requires it. If a loan is cancelled, reversed or repaid early, revise or cancel the commission as the agreement provides.

Schedule partner follow-up

Do not record OTPs, card PINs, net-banking passwords or full account numbers in follow-up notes.
