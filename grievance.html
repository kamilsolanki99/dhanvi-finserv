Step 2 - Our response

We will acknowledge your complaint and keep you updated on its progress. If we need more information or additional time to investigate, we will contact you and explain why. Response and resolution times can vary with the lender and the nature of the complaint.

Step 3 - Escalation

If the matter is not resolved to your satisfaction, ask us to escalate it to a senior team member. Include the original complaint and any reference number you received.

Step 4 - Lender grievance process

For issues relating to a bank or NBFC - such as a sanction, disbursal, statement or charge - you may also use that lender's official grievance redressal channel. We will help you with the relevant contact details and documents.

Regulatory recourse

If your complaint relates to a regulated lender and remains unresolved after the lender's internal process, you may approach the appropriate banking ombudsman or regulator as per applicable rules. We will cooperate with any such process.

Record keeping

We maintain a record of complaints and their resolution for internal review and quality improvement.
