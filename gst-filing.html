Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions answered regarding GST Registration & Filing.

Who is required to register for GST?

Businesses selling goods with annual turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs (₹20 Lakhs for services), businesses engaged in inter-state supply, or e-commerce sellers are mandated to register.

Can I obtain voluntary GST registration?

Yes. Many early-stage enterprises take voluntary registration to claim input tax credit on purchases and build credibility for bank business loans.

What happens if GST returns are filed late?

Late filing attracts a statutory late fee of ₹50 per day (₹20 for NIL return) plus 18% p.a. interest on unpaid net tax liabilities.

How does GST compliance help my loan application?

Banks and NBFCs directly fetch your 12-month GSTR-3B filings to verify declared business turnover when evaluating business and working capital loans.
