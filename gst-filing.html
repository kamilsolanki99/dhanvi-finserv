GST Registration & Return Filing, Handled Properly

From a first GSTIN to monthly returns, amendments, cancellations and pending-compliance clean-ups, we prepare the filing, explain the tax position and act only after your approval.

GST compliance affects your working capital, input tax credit, bank loan eligibility and ability to bid for tenders. A late return or a mismatch can trigger interest, late fees and blocked credit, so the registration category and return frequency need to be correct from the start.

We support proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs and companies with new registration, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing, QRMP and composition schemes, annual returns, amendments, cancellations, notice responses and ITC reconciliation. Who Needs GST Registration?

Registration is mandatory in several situations and optional in others. These are the common triggers - we confirm your exact position before applying.

Turnover thresholds

Goods businesses generally cross the threshold at ₹40 lakh and services at ₹20 lakh, with lower limits in special-category states. Voluntary registration is available below the threshold.

Inter-state & e-commerce

Inter-state taxable supplies, e-commerce operators and sellers, and certain notified categories require registration regardless of turnover.

Business & tender needs

Banks, NBFCs and government tenders commonly ask for a GSTIN and filed returns. Voluntary registration can also unlock input tax credit on purchases.

Composition scheme, QRMP and voluntary registration have different eligibility conditions. We review your turnover, supply type and state before recommending an option.

Documents Required

The exact list depends on your constitution and the GST service selected. These are the common documents to start with.

Identity & signatory

  • PAN card of the business and signatory
  • Aadhaar card (masked reference stored)
  • Passport-size photographs

Constitution

  • Certificate of incorporation, for companies
  • Partnership deed, for firms and LLPs

Place of business

  • Electricity bill or property tax receipt

Returns & financials

  • Previous GST returns, if registered
  • GST certificate or registration copy
  • Bank statements and financial statements

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions answered regarding GST Registration & Filing.

Who is required to register for GST?

Registration is generally required when aggregate turnover crosses the threshold - commonly ₹40 lakh for goods and ₹20 lakh for services, with lower limits in special-category states. It is also required for inter-state taxable supplies, e-commerce operators and sellers, and certain notified categories, irrespective of turnover. The application captures your turnover, supply type and state so we can confirm the correct position.

Can I obtain voluntary GST registration?

Yes. Many early-stage enterprises take voluntary registration to claim input tax credit on purchases and to build credibility for business loans, working capital and tenders. The application includes a voluntary registration option, and we review whether the compliance cost is worthwhile for your turnover and customer profile.

Does submitting the application register my business or file my return?

No. Submitting the application creates a GST assistance request with the status Application Received. We verify your documents, prepare the registration, amendment, cancellation or return, and share it for approval. The action is completed on the GST portal only after your approval, and your status becomes Filed & Verified only when the ARN or approval is actually confirmed.

What happens if GST returns are filed late?

Late filing attracts a statutory late fee per day, plus interest on unpaid net tax liability at the prescribed rate. Input tax credit can also be affected and pending returns can block refunds or new registrations. The exact fee and interest depend on the return type and period, so we confirm the position before filing.

How is my Aadhaar and bank data protected?

Aadhaar is optional at the application stage; if provided, only a masked reference is stored and the full number is not retained in drafts. Bank account numbers are masked in drafts and stored only as needed for the application. Documents are uploaded to a private storage bucket and are not exposed through public URLs. We never ask for a GST portal password, OTP or DSC PIN on this website.

How does GST compliance help my loan application?

Banks and NBFCs commonly review GST returns and turnover when evaluating business and working capital loans. Consistent filings, a clean GSTIN and reconciled input tax credit support the assessment. GST compliance does not guarantee a loan approval or a specific amount - the lender's credit policy and your overall profile decide the outcome.
