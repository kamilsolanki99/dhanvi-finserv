### D1. Product details

**Home Loan**

- Purpose: purchase, construction, plot purchase, renovation.
- Funding: up to 90% of property value.
- Tenure: up to 30 years.
- Applicants: salaried, self-employed, and NRIs.
- Eligibility: minimum age 21; loan should close by lender’s retirement age; minimum net monthly income ₹15,000; CIBIL 700+ preferred.
- Documents: PAN, Aadhaar, photographs, address proof, salary slips/Form 16 or 2–3 years ITR, 6–12 months bank statements, property title/approval/tax/NOC documents.
- Rate: the home-loan landing page says **9.70% p.a. onwards**. This is marketing copy, not a current personalised quote.

**Home Loan Top-Up**

- Requires an existing home loan with a satisfactory repayment record.
- Can be used for renovation, interiors, business, education, or personal needs.
- Typically priced lower than an unsecured personal loan.
- Repaid alongside the existing home loan, usually with one EMI.
- No separate maximum or fee is published.

**Home Loan Takeover**

- Moves an existing home loan to a new lender at a lower rate.
- Can reduce EMI, shorten tenure, or include a top-up.
- Dhanvi coordinates NOC, existing-loan documents, new sanction, and disbursal.
- No guaranteed saving, processing fee, or legal cost is published.

**Loan Against Property**

- Secured against residential, commercial, or industrial property.
- Use cases include business funding, education, marriage, and major expenses.
- Tenure up to 15 years.
- Eligibility depends on income and ownership; lender values the property.
- No LTV percentage or rate is published.

**Education Loan**

- For studies in India or abroad.
- Covers tuition, hostel, and living expenses.
- Repayment usually begins after the course.
- Claims include 100% finance, a ₹10 lakh unsecured option, 2,000+ customers, subsidy schemes for selected courses, and insurance for loan liability.
- The minimum-age, minimum-income, and “salaried/self-employed” boilerplate on this page appears copied from other products and should not be applied literally to student borrowers.
- Section 80E tax benefit is mentioned; it is subject to tax law and the borrower’s circumstances.

**Vehicle Loan**

- New cars, pre-owned cars, two-wheelers, and loans against cars.
- The product page says funding up to 100%; the services page and homepage say up to 90%.
- The vehicle landing page advertises 8.70% for a new car and 9.70% for a used car.
- “Loan against car” says no income documents are required; this is a site claim that needs lender confirmation.
- Dealer quote/proforma invoice, vehicle details, KYC, income proof, and bank statements are commonly requested.

**Business Loan**

- Working capital, machinery, expansion, and other business needs.
- Secured and unsecured options.
- For proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, and private limited companies.
- Business vintage generally 1–3 years, subject to lender.
- FAQ says up to ₹50 lakh; no rate or fee schedule is published.
- The generic eligibility block says “salaried and self-employed,” which is not appropriate for a pure business-loan audience.

**Personal Loan**

- Unsecured; no collateral.
- Use cases: medical, wedding, travel, education, consumer durables.
- Tenure 1–6 years.
- FAQ limits: ₹50,000–₹25 lakh.
- Minimum income shown as ₹15,000; CIBIL 700+ preferred.
- No processing fee or rate is published.

**ITR Filing**

- Audience: salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, business owners, and mixed-income filers.
- Scope: document review, return preparation, computation review, e-filing, and acknowledgement.
- Can review prior-year, belated, and revised returns; eligibility depends on tax rules and deadlines.
- The page explicitly says a refund is **not guaranteed**.
- The fee is confirmed after the income sources and return type are reviewed.
- No payment is collected on the page.
- No tax professional qualification, registration, or legal entity is disclosed.

## G. Process, Documents, and Timelines

### G1. Standard loan journey

1. **Share requirement:** call, WhatsApp, or submit an enquiry.
2. **Documents and eligibility:** Dhanvi provides a checklist and checks options with lenders.
3. **Compare and verify:** compare rates/terms and complete lender verification.
4. **Sanction and disbursal:** sign the sanction letter, complete formalities, receive funds.

### G2. Published timelines

- Sanction after complete documents: typically **2–7 working days**.
- Disbursal after sanction and formalities: usually **2–5 working days**.
- Secured loans can take longer because of legal and technical checks.
- Sanction validity: usually **30–90 days**, lender-dependent.
- These are indicative and must not be converted into guarantees.

### G3. Document checklist

**Identity and address**

- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Passport-size photographs
- Address proof: utility bill, rent agreement, or passport

**Income proof — salaried**

- Last 3 months’ salary slips
- Last 6 months’ bank statements
- Form 16 or latest ITR
- Employee ID or offer letter

**Income proof — self-employed**

- Last 2–3 years’ ITR with computation
- Profit and loss account and balance sheet
- Last 12 months’ bank statements
- Business proof such as GST, Udyam, or registration

**Property documents**

- Sale deed or agreement to sell
- Approved building plan and layout
- Property tax receipt
- NOC from society or builder
- Existing loan statement for takeover
- Payment receipts and chain of title

**Vehicle documents**

- Proforma invoice or dealer quotation
- Dealer and vehicle details
- Driving licence for some lenders
- Existing loan statement for refinance

**Education documents**

- Student KYC and photographs
- Co-applicant KYC and income proof
- Admission letter and fee structure
- Class 10, 12, and graduation marksheets
- Entrance exam documents

**Business documents**

- Business address proof
- Last 2 years’ ITR, financials, and computation
- Last 12 months’ bank statements
- GST returns and Udyam/shop licence
- Ownership proof or rent agreement
- Current account statement and existing loan statements
- Partner details, if applicable

The exact list depends on lender, loan type, income profile, and property/vehicle/course. Always say the lender may request more documents.

### G4. ITR process

1. Share income type and financial year.
2. Review the document checklist.
3. Prepare the return and share the computation for review.
4. E-file and provide the acknowledgement.

The site says filing is subject to document verification, applicable tax rules, and availability of the Income Tax e-filing portal.

### H2. Eligibility calculator

**Inputs:**

- Required loan amount
- Net monthly income
- Existing monthly loan commitments
- Loan tenure in years
- Interest rate (default 9.5%)

**Logic:**

- FOIR is 40% for income up to ₹14,999, 45% for ₹15,000–₹29,999, and 50% for ₹30,000+.
- It deducts only 40% of existing EMIs, not the full EMI.
- It calculates the present value of the affordable EMI over the requested tenure.
- It compares the maximum eligible amount with the requested amount.

**Limitations:**

- Very rough; can overstate eligibility because of the partial EMI deduction.
- No credit score, age, employment type, obligations, or lender policy.
- No validation of unrealistic inputs.
- Never present its output as approval.

## P. Technical, Forms, Privacy, and Compliance

**Stack:** static HTML, Bootstrap, jQuery, Owl Carousel, custom CSS, custom JavaScript. No server-side application is included in the local project.

**Forms:** 53 instances across 31 pages.

- 31 newsletter forms; action `https://jituchauhan.com/borrow/bootstrap-4/newsletter.php`.
- 18 forms with blank actions: service quote forms, blog comment forms, search/filter forms, and the document wizard.
- 2 landing-page quote forms; action `https://jituchauhan.com/borrow/bootstrap-4/get-quote.php`.
- 1 contact form; action `https://jituchauhan.com/borrow/bootstrap-4/contact-us.php`.
- 1 ITR form; action `#`.

**JavaScript interception:** `js/wa-forms.js` attaches to every form whose action is blank, `#`, or contains `jituchauhan`. On submit it prevents the default action, builds a WhatsApp message from named fields, and opens `https://wa.me/919925002324`.

**Implications:**

- There is no first-party backend, CRM, email delivery, or database in the local project.
- If JavaScript is disabled, forms can post to `jituchauhan.com`, a third-party domain.
- The contact form’s fallback sends name, email, phone, and message to that third party.
- The newsletter has no double opt-in, consent language, or visible privacy link.
- There is no CAPTCHA, rate limit, CSRF protection, server-side validation, or spam control because there is no server application.
- No consent checkbox is present on any form.
- The generic interceptor also attaches to non-enquiry forms such as blog search and the document wizard; test for unintended WhatsApp launches.
- WhatsApp prefilled messages include whatever fields have names, which may include personal contact details.

**Privacy policy claims:**

- Collects contact details, loan requirement details, documents, browser/device information, and cookie/server-log data.
- Uses data for enquiries, lender comparison, documentation, support, improvement, and legal compliance.
- May share data with banks, NBFCs, credit bureaus, and service providers.
- Says it does not sell personal information.
- Offers access/correction/deletion requests and consent withdrawal.
- Contact is the same phone and personal Hotmail address.
- No legal entity, registered address, DPO, or grievance officer is identified.

**Compliance risks:**

- No consent capture before sending data to WhatsApp or a third party.
- No data-processing agreement or privacy notice at the point of collection.
- No secure document-upload channel is described.
- No commission disclosure.
- No licence or registration details for the loan consultancy.
- No tax-professional credentials for ITR filing.
- Potential misleading claims (“instant e-approval,” “lowest rates,” “no hidden charges”).
- The site says “no cost to you” while lender fees exist and Dhanvi’s commission is undisclosed.
- The privacy policy mentions cookies and logs, but no cookie tool or analytics was found.

**Security:** Because the local build is static, server security controls are not present. Before launch, add HTTPS, security headers, a first-party form endpoint, spam protection, consent logging, retention rules, and access controls.
