Apply for GST assistance

Complete this secure multi-step application for a new GST registration, return filing, amendment, cancellation or ongoing compliance support. Your progress can be saved and resumed, and documents are uploaded through a private link after submission. Submitting this form starts an assistance request - it does not register a business or file a return with the GST portal.

Step 1 of 7

Applicant & place of business

Enter the applicant or authorised signatory details exactly as they appear on the PAN and business records. We store only a masked Aadhaar reference and never ask for an Aadhaar OTP on this page.

Aadhaar authentication is required for most new GST registrations, subject to the exemptions notified by the GST authorities. We never store your full Aadhaar number.

Step 2 of 7

Business profile

Tell us about the legal entity, its constitution and the activity it carries on. This determines the documents and the registration category.

Turnover means aggregate turnover across all PAN-linked registrations in the same state. If you are unsure, enter your best estimate; we verify it before filing.

Step 3 of 7

Service requirement

Select the GST service you need. The fields below change based on your selection. For an existing GSTIN, we use the number captured in your business profile.

GSTIN on record

Step 4 of 7

Sales, purchases & tax position

Enter the figures for the period relevant to your request. These are indicative and are reconciled with your invoices, returns and books before any filing. Nil figures are allowed.

Indicative net tax position

Enter the liability, tax paid and late fee to see an indicative net payable. This is not a GST computation and does not confirm any liability, credit or refund. Final figures are reconciled by our tax team.

Step 5 of 7

Authorised signatory & bank details

The authorised signatory must be the proprietor, partner, director, Karta or an authorised representative. Bank details must match the business bank account used for GST refunds and payments.

Business bank account

Only a masked account number is stored. We may ask for a cancelled cheque or bank statement during document verification.

Step 6 of 7

Document checklist

This list is based on your answers. After submitting, you will receive a private upload link. Upload clear PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP files (maximum 5 MB each).

Documents we will need

Keep these ready

PAN and Aadhaar, photographs of the proprietor / partners / directors, address proof of the principal place of business, rent agreement or NOC, business registration proof, bank proof, sales and purchase invoices, previous GST returns and the GST certificate where applicable. We confirm the final list after reviewing your registration and service type.

Step 7 of 7

Review & consent

Check your details before submitting. This creates a GST assistance request; it does not register your business or file a return. Registration, amendments, cancellations and returns are submitted only after document verification, your approval and actual action on the GST portal.

Dhanvi Finserv is an assistance service, not the GST Department. Registration approval, return processing, refunds and notices are controlled by the GST authorities. Never share GST portal passwords, OTPs or DSC PINs over WhatsApp or email.
