Eligibility Criteria

General borrower requirements. Final approval and terms depend on lender assessment and your profile.

Fleet Operators & Transporters

Minimum 1 to 2 years experience in transportation or existing ownership of at least one commercial vehicle.

Manufacturing & Contracting Units

Active industrial manufacturing, subcontracting, or civil works vintage of at least 2 years.

Credit & Operational Parameters

Satisfactory repayment track record, valid commercial permits/licenses, and healthy bank turnover.

Documents Required

Keep these documents ready to ensure instant verification and rapid sanction.

KYC Proofs

  • PAN Card & Aadhaar Card
  • Business registration (GST/Udyam)
  • Commercial driving license (for small operators)

Financial Credentials

  • 2 years ITR with computation
  • 6 to 12 months bank statements
  • Existing vehicle RC copies / fleet list

Asset Quotation

  • Proforma invoice from authorized dealer
  • Machine specification & supplier quote
  • Road permits & operational contracts (if applicable)

Lenders may request specific additional documentation depending on your borrower category and requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Commercial Vehicle & Machinery.

Can I finance a pre-owned or used commercial vehicle?

Yes. We arrange finance for used commercial vehicles and second-hand industrial machinery subject to technical valuation and vehicle age criteria.

Does the loan cover body building and insurance costs?

Certain lenders provide comprehensive funding packages that include chassis value, insurance premium, and approved body fabrication costs.

Can first-time vehicle buyers apply?

Yes. First-time buyers (FTBs) with good driver experience or a co-applicant/guarantor with stable banking can obtain vehicle finance.

Is collateral required beyond the vehicle/machine itself?

In most standard asset finance schemes, the commercial vehicle or machinery purchased serves as the primary hypothecated collateral; no extra property is required.
