About Professional Loan

Exclusive, high-ticket unsecured credit lines customized for Doctors, Chartered Accountants, Architects, and Company Secretaries to expand clinics, offices, and practices.

As a certified professional, your credentials and practice revenue command premium standing with banks. Professional loans recognize your professional degree and earning trajectory with preferential terms.

Dhanvi Finserv coordinates with specialized professional lending desks of premier private and nationalized banks, unlocking unsecured loans up to ₹50 Lakhs with minimum documentation and zero collateral.

Our dedicated loan experts in Ahmedabad guide you end-to-end: lender comparison, valuation, documentation, swift sanction, and smooth disbursal.

Eligibility Criteria

General borrower requirements. Final approval and terms depend on lender assessment and your profile.

Medical Professionals

MBBS, MD, MS, BDS, MDS with valid Medical Council of India / State Medical Council registration and minimum 2 years post-degree practice.

Chartered Accountants

Qualified Chartered Accountants with active ICAI Certificate of Practice (COP) for a minimum of 2 continuous years.

Architects & Engineers

Council of Architecture (COA) registration or certified engineering credential with demonstrated independent practice history.

Documents Required

Keep these documents ready to ensure instant verification and rapid sanction.

Professional Credentials

  • Degree Certificate & Final Marks Sheet
  • State Council / ICAI / COA Registration
  • Certificate of Practice (COP)

KYC Documents

  • PAN Card of applicant
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Proof of clinic / office address

Financial Records

  • Last 6 to 12 months primary bank statement
  • Latest 2 years Income Tax Returns (ITR)
  • Existing loan sanction letters (if any)

Lenders may request specific additional documentation depending on your borrower category and requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Professional Loan.

Can non-practicing salaried doctors apply?

Yes. Salaried medical consultants and doctors employed with government or private hospitals are eligible under our salaried professional loan program.

Can partnership clinics or LLPs apply?

Yes. Professional partnership firms, LLPs, and private healthcare companies where qualified professionals hold majority equity are fully eligible.

Are there restrictions on how the funds are used?

Funds can be utilized for clinic setup, diagnostic machinery, office expansion, technology acquisition, debt consolidation, or personal liquidity.

How quickly can a professional loan be sanctioned?

With digital KYC and bank statement uploads, preliminary approval is often generated within 24 hours, followed by prompt disbursal.
