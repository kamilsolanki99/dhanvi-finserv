Statutory Recognition for Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises

Unlock lower interest rates, priority sector bank lending, government tender preferences, and statutory protection against delayed payments.

Udyam Registration is the government's official identity for MSMEs in India. It links directly with Income Tax and GST databases to classify your enterprise as Micro, Small, or Medium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions answered regarding MSME / Udyam Registration.

What are the revised MSME classification limits?

Micro: Investment < ₹1 Cr & Turnover < ₹5 Cr. Small: Investment < ₹10 Cr & Turnover < ₹50 Cr. Medium: Investment < ₹50 Cr & Turnover < ₹250 Cr.

Is Udyam Registration mandatory for bank business loans?

Yes. Almost all public and private sector banks insist on valid Udyam registration to book the borrowing under RBI Priority Sector Lending (PSL).

How long does it take to obtain the certificate?

With correct Aadhaar-linked OTP verification, the certificate is usually generated within 24 to 48 hours.

Is there an annual renewal required for Udyam?

No. Udyam registration carries lifetime validity. Only annual update of investment and turnover data from filed ITR/GST is required.
