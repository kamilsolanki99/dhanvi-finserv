Udyam, Gumasta & Business Registration, Handled Properly

Get statutory recognition for your micro, small or medium enterprise, complete an Udyam update, or obtain a Shop & Establishment (Gumasta) licence with document-level support from Dhanvi Finserv.

Udyam Registration is the Government of India's identity for micro, small and medium enterprises. It links your PAN, GSTIN and business details, and supports priority-sector lending, subsidy schemes, tender participation and statutory protection against delayed payments.

We help entrepreneurs across Ahmedabad and Gujarat with new Udyam registration, Udyam amendments, municipal Shop & Establishment (Gumasta) licences and business registration assistance - including promoter details, premises documents and portal acknowledgements.

What this service is. Dhanvi Finserv is an assistance and documentation service. We prepare, verify and submit applications on the relevant government portal after your approval. Registration approval, certificate issuance, fees and penalties are controlled by the Udyam portal, the municipal corporation or the registrar concerned.

Who Should Register?

Udyam and Gumasta serve different purposes. Many businesses need both.

Manufacturers, Traders & Service Providers

Any micro, small or medium enterprise - manufacturing, trading or services - can obtain Udyam registration using its PAN, GSTIN and business details.

Businesses Seeking Loans or Subsidies

Banks and NBFCs commonly ask for Udyam registration for MSME lending, CGTMSE-backed loans, MUDRA schemes and priority-sector classification.

Shops & Commercial Establishments

A Gumasta / Shop & Establishment licence is a state or municipal registration required for many shops, restaurants, offices and commercial premises.

Existing Udyam Holders

Use the Udyam update service when your activity, address, ownership, investment, turnover or employee count has changed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions about Udyam, Gumasta and business registration assistance.

What are the revised MSME classification limits?

Micro: investment up to ₹2.5 crore and annual turnover up to ₹10 crore. Small: investment up to ₹25 crore and turnover up to ₹100 crore. Medium: investment up to ₹125 crore and turnover up to ₹500 crore. Both tests must be satisfied. The Udyam portal determines the final category from verified records and the latest government notification.

Is Udyam Registration required for bank business loans?

Many public and private sector banks ask for valid Udyam registration to classify MSME lending, offer priority-sector benefits or process CGTMSE / MUDRA-backed facilities. The exact requirement and pricing depend on the lender and the scheme. Dhanvi Finserv can coordinate both the registration and the loan application, but loan approval is always at the lender's discretion.

How long does it take to obtain the certificate?

Timelines depend on document readiness, Aadhaar / PAN validation, portal availability and any government query. A straightforward Udyam application can be generated quickly once the details and OTP verification are complete; Gumasta and other registrations vary by municipal or registrar requirements. We do not promise a fixed approval date because the decision rests with the government authority.

Is there an annual renewal required for Udyam?

Udyam registration does not require a yearly renewal, but the portal expects the enterprise to keep its investment, turnover and other details updated, including the annual updation required by the Udyam framework. Use the Udyam update service whenever your business details change.

What is the difference between Udyam and Gumasta?

Udyam is the national MSME registration issued through the Ministry of MSME portal. Gumasta, also called Shop & Establishment registration, is a state or municipal licence for a shop or commercial establishment under the applicable state law. They are separate registrations with different authorities, documents and fees, and many businesses need both.

Does submitting this application mean my business is registered?

No. Submitting the form creates an MSME assistance request with the status Application Received. It does not register your enterprise, update Udyam, issue a Gumasta licence or complete any government registration. Our administrator moves the status to Approved & Verified only after the actual action on the relevant government portal and verification of the certificate or acknowledgement. Always check the certificate number and portal reference before relying on the registration.
