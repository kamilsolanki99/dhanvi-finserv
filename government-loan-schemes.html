About Govt Schemes (MUDRA/CGTMSE)

Harness Government of India credit guarantees to launch, sustain, or expand your micro, small, or medium enterprise without pledging ancestral or personal property.

Government schemes like Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) and CGTMSE were formulated to bridge the funding gap for small traders, service providers, and manufacturing entrepreneurs.

Navigating bank scheme guidelines, project report preparation, and eligibility documentation can be intimidating. Dhanvi Finserv prepares your dossier to ensure smooth bank sanction under the appropriate scheme.

Our dedicated loan experts in Ahmedabad guide you end-to-end: lender comparison, valuation, documentation, swift sanction, and smooth disbursal.

Options Tailored For Your Requirements

Choose the optimal funding structure that aligns precisely with your financial objectives.

PMMY Shishu Scheme

Loans up to ₹50,000 for tiny micro-enterprises, rural businesses, and self-employed street vendors with negligible paperwork.

PMMY Kishore Scheme

Funding from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000 for purchasing equipment, retail store inventory, or basic business setup.

PMMY Tarun Scheme

Capital from ₹5,00,000 up to ₹10,00,000 (and extended up to ₹20 Lakhs) for established small manufacturing and service units.

CGTMSE Credit Guarantee

Collateral-free bank borrowing up to ₹5 Crores for MSMEs where the credit guarantee trust covers bank default risk.

Eligibility Criteria

General borrower requirements. Final approval and terms depend on lender assessment and your profile.

Eligible Activities

Non-farm micro, small, and medium enterprises involved in manufacturing, processing, trading, or retail service sectors.

Registration Credentials

Valid Udyam (MSME) registration, local municipal shop establishment (Gumasta), and active bank account.

Applicant Age & Nationality

Indian citizens aged 18 years and above with viable business plan and clear non-defaulter banking history.

Documents Required

Keep these documents ready to ensure instant verification and rapid sanction.

Identity & Business Proof

  • Aadhaar Card & PAN Card
  • Udyam Registration Certificate
  • Shop Establishment / Municipal License
  • Partnership / Company Deeds

Business Project Plan

  • Detailed Project Report (DPR)
  • Estimated machinery / asset quotes
  • Projected balance sheets & P&L

Banking & Past Filings

  • Bank statement for the last 6 months
  • ITR of promoters (if filed previously)
  • Tax clearance / GST certificates (if registered)

Lenders may request specific additional documentation depending on your borrower category and requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Govt Schemes (MUDRA/CGTMSE).

Does Dhanvi Finserv provide the government loan directly?

No. Dhanvi Finserv is an independent loan consultancy. We consult, prepare project reports, evaluate eligibility, and arrange sanction through partner public and private sector banks.

Is a collateral guarantee completely waived under CGTMSE?

Yes. Lenders are covered by the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE), eliminating the requirement of external collateral for eligible amounts.

Can women entrepreneurs get preferential terms?

Under the Stand-Up India and specific state women enterprise quotas, women founders access loans from ₹10 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore with lower promoter margins.

What is the MUDRA Card?

The MUDRA Card is a RuPay debit facility provided against your approved working capital limit to draw funds and purchase inventory as required.
