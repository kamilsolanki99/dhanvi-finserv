Lead allocation is not guaranteed

Dhanvi Finserv allocates leads based on customer location, product fit, partner capacity, verification status and lender requirements. A partner application or agreement does not guarantee any volume of leads, a sanction, a disbursal or a commission.

Step 4 of 5

Document checklist

After submitting, you will receive a private upload link. Upload clear PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP files (maximum 5 MB each). The verification team reviews every document before approval.

Documents likely required for your profile

Upload only through the private link

PAN, address proof, photograph, business registration and onboarding documents contain personal and business information. Do not send them over WhatsApp, email or a public URL. The private upload link is tied to your application reference and access token.

What happens after submission

The team reviews the application, verifies the documents, records the verification outcome and, if approved, prepares the partner agreement. A partner login is provisioned only after approval and a signed, effective agreement.

Step 5 of 5

Review & declarations

Check your details before submitting. This creates a partner onboarding application. It does not approve you as a partner, guarantee leads or commission, or create a binding agency appointment.

Approval, agreement and login provisioning are separate steps. Do not represent yourself as an authorised Dhanvi Finserv partner until you receive written confirmation and an active partner login. Never share customer OTPs, card PINs or portal passwords.
