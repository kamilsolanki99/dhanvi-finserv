About Working Capital (CC/OD)

Ensure uninterrupted operations, finance seasonal surges, and clear vendor liabilities with revolving credit lines customized to your business cycle.

Unlike standard term loans where interest accumulates on the full amount, Cash Credit and Overdraft facilities allow you to withdraw funds as needed and pay interest solely on the daily utilized balance.

Dhanvi Finserv arranges both hypothecated limits against current assets (stocks & debtors) and secured overdraft facilities against commercial, industrial, or residential real estate across Gujarat.

Our dedicated loan experts in Ahmedabad guide you end-to-end: lender comparison, valuation, documentation, swift sanction, and smooth disbursal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Working Capital (CC/OD).

What is the core difference between CC and OD?

Cash Credit (CC) is typically backed by current assets such as stock and book debts, whereas Overdraft (OD) is often backed by financial deposits or immovable property.

Can I enhance my CC/OD limit later?

Yes. During your annual facility review, our advisors help you compile audited revenue numbers and request limit expansions from your lender.

Is collateral compulsory for working capital?

Unsecured working capital lines are available up to ₹50 Lakhs for eligible MSMEs. Limits exceeding this threshold generally require property or collateral security.

How is the drawing power (DP) calculated?

Drawing Power is calculated monthly from your current stock value minus unpaid creditors, plus acceptable trade debtors within 90-120 days, factoring in standard bank margins.

What is the difference between a new facility, enhancement, renewal and takeover?

A new facility is a fresh CC/OD limit. An enhancement increases an existing limit. A renewal extends the current facility for another review period. A takeover moves an existing facility from one lender to another, subject to the new lender's approval and closure of the old account.

Can I apply if I do not have an existing CC/OD facility?

Yes. Select “New CC/OD Facility” and choose “None” for the existing facility. The application will still ask for your financials, banking history and proposed security so lenders can assess the request.

How long does a working capital sanction take?

Timelines vary by lender and profile. A complete application with GST returns, ITR, financial statements, bank statements and stock/debtors data usually helps avoid delays. Your advisor will share the expected timeline after reviewing the file.
