Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Personal Loan Balance Transfer.

How much money will I really save by transferring?

A reduction of just 2-3% on a ₹10 Lakh personal loan across 3-4 years can save you ₹50,000 to ₹1,20,000 in interest even after factoring in transfer processing fees.

Will the new bank pay my existing lender directly?

Yes. Once sanctioned, the new lender issues an electronic RTGS payment or banker's cheque directly to your current bank to settle and close the old facility.

Are there foreclosure charges on my existing loan?

Under RBI guidelines, floating-rate loans to individuals carry zero foreclosure charges. Fixed-rate personal loans may incur a small pre-closure fee of 2-4%.

Can I get an additional loan amount during transfer?

Yes! This is known as a Balance Transfer Top-Up. The extra funds are credited directly to your savings bank account upon completion.
