ITR Filing Services Made Easy

Get clear, professional support for your income tax return - from document review to e-filing.

Whether you are a salaried employee, a self-employed professional or a business owner, we help you understand what is required and prepare your return accurately.

File Your ITR WhatsApp

Who can file with us?

  • Salaried individuals - Form 16, salary income and eligible deductions.
  • Self-employed professionals - professional income, expenses and presumptive tax options.
  • Business owners - business income, expenses and applicable tax records.

The right documents and ITR form depend on your income sources. We confirm these before filing.

Already Repaying a Loan? We're Still Here to Help

Post-disbursal support for existing Dhanvi Finserv customers - from repayment schedules and foreclosure queries to balance transfer, top-up and annual loan reviews.

If your loan is already disbursed, you do not need to start a new application. Use our existing-customer support desk to raise your request online and receive a reference number for follow-up.

Support for existing customers

  • Repayment schedule - request a copy of your repayment schedule.

  • Balance transfer or top-up - review options on your existing loan.
