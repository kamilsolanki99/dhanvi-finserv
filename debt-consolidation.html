About Debt Consolidation

Stop juggling 4 or 5 different EMIs with punishing 18% to 40% interest rates. Consolidate your debts into a single, low-interest long-tenure loan.

When multiple high-cost obligations (credit card balances, short-term personal loans, consumer loans) pile up, monthly interest burns a huge hole in your finances and threatens your credit score.

At Dhanvi Finserv, we help you leverage your residential, commercial, or industrial property to secure a low-interest Loan Against Property (LAP) that clears all unsecured debts in one sweep.

Our dedicated loan experts in Ahmedabad guide you end-to-end: lender comparison, valuation, documentation, swift sanction, and smooth disbursal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Debt Consolidation.

How does debt consolidation through property mortgage work?

The bank sanctions a Loan Against Property. Instead of disbursing funds to you, the lender directly issues settlement cheques to clear all your existing high-interest loans, leaving you with one single low-rate EMI.

Will debt consolidation hurt my CIBIL score?

No, quite the opposite! Settling multiple outstanding cards and loans significantly drops your credit utilization ratio and eliminates the risk of missed payment dates, strengthening your score over time.

What if I don't own property?

If you don't own property, we can explore an unsecured debt consolidation loan up to ₹25 Lakhs, depending on your salary, employer tier, and credit profile.

Can Dhanvi Finserv calculate my exact monthly savings?

Absolutely. Bring us your latest loan statements and credit card bills. Our advisors will map your current total outgo against the proposed consolidated EMI to show your exact monthly savings in writing.
