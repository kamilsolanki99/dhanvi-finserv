Documents required for a loan

Requirements differ slightly by lender, loan type and income profile. Use the checklists below to prepare your file in advance - it is the fastest way to a clean approval.

Already applied? Upload PAN, Aadhaar, salary slips, ITR and bank statements through our secure document upload - files go directly to private storage and are never requested over WhatsApp or email.
Existing customer? Request a repayment schedule, foreclosure statement, part payment or annual loan review through post-disbursal support.

Self-attested copies

Submit clear photocopies signed by you. Carry originals only for verification - lenders return them after checking.

Digital applications

Scans should be in PDF or JPG, complete and readable. Name each file clearly, for example PAN_Kamil.pdf.

Keep it current

Bank statements and salary slips should be recent - usually the last 3 to 6 months. Old files slow down verification.

Home Loan documents

Identity & address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof (utility bill, rent agreement or passport)

Income proof

Salaried

  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16 or latest ITR
  • Employee ID / offer letter

Self-employed

  • Last 2-3 years ITR with computation
  • Profit & loss and balance sheet
  • Last 12 months bank statement
  • Business proof (GST / Udyam / registration)

Property documents

  • Sale deed or agreement to sell
  • Approved building plan and layout
  • Property tax receipt
  • NOC from society or builder
  • Existing loan statement (for takeover)
  • Payment receipts / chain of title

The lender may arrange a technical and legal valuation of the property.

Loan Against Property documents

Identity & address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof

Income proof

Salaried

  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16 or latest ITR

Self-employed

  • Last 2-3 years ITR with financials
  • Last 12 months bank statement
  • Business proof (GST / Udyam / registration)

Property documents

  • Title deed and chain of documents
  • Latest property tax receipt
  • Occupancy certificate / approved plan
  • Existing mortgage or loan details
  • Property valuation (arranged by lender)

Business Loan documents

Identity & address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Business address proof

Business & income proof

  • Last 2 years ITR with computation
  • Audited financials / P&L and balance sheet
  • Last 12 months bank statement
  • GST returns and Udyam / shop licence
  • GST / VAT registration certificate

Business-specific

  • Ownership proof or rent agreement of premises
  • Current account statement
  • Existing loan statements
  • Details of business partners (if any)

Lenders may ask for stock statements or GST-based turnover reports for larger amounts.

Personal Loan documents

Identity & address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof

Income proof

Salaried

  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16 or latest ITR
  • Employee ID / offer letter

Self-employed

  • Last 2 years ITR
  • Last 6-12 months bank statement
  • Business proof (GST / Udyam / registration)

Additional documents

  • Existing loan or credit card statements
  • Purpose of loan (if asked by lender)
  • Reference details (as requested)

A personal loan can usually be approved with minimal paperwork and a shorter tenure than secured loans.

Education Loan documents

Student KYC

  • Student PAN / Aadhaar
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof
  • Co-applicant PAN and Aadhaar

Co-applicant income

Salaried

  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16 or ITR

Self-employed

  • Last 2-3 years ITR
  • Business financials
  • Last 12 months bank statement

Course & institution

  • Admission letter
  • Fee structure / fee receipt
  • Class 10, 12 and graduation marksheets
  • Entrance exam score card
  • Collateral documents (for secured loans)

Vehicle Loan documents

Identity & address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof

Income proof

Salaried

  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16 or latest ITR

Self-employed

  • Last 2 years ITR
  • Last 6-12 months bank statement
  • Business proof (GST / Udyam / registration)

Vehicle documents

  • Proforma invoice / quotation from dealer
  • Dealer and vehicle details
  • Driving licence (for some lenders)
  • Existing vehicle loan statement (for refinance)
