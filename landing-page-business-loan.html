What is the minimum business vintage needed to qualify?

Most lenders require at least 2 to 3 years of continuous operational track record backed by filed Income Tax Returns.

Can proprietary firms and partnerships apply?

Yes! Proprietorships, partnership firms, LLPs, and private limited companies are fully eligible.

Is collateral mandatory for a business loan?

No. Unsecured business loans up to ₹50 Lakhs require no property or collateral. Amounts exceeding ₹50 Lakhs can be secured against property, machinery, or liquid assets.

How does Dhanvi Finserv assist in the approval process?

We audit your financials, prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPR), negotiate the lowest interest rate across 30+ lenders, and coordinate with the sanctioning authority.
