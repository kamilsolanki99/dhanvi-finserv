Eligibility Criteria

General borrower requirements. Final approval and terms depend on lender assessment and your profile.

Age Requirement

Any Indian citizen aged 18 to 75 years holding valid government photo identification.

Gold Ownership

Gold ornaments or jewellery of 18 to 22 carats purity. Hallmarked jewellery is readily accepted.

Income & Credit History

No stringent CIBIL score or formal salary slip requirements. Approval is primarily backed by physical collateral valuation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about Gold Loan.

How is the gold loan amount calculated?

The loan amount is determined by the net weight and purity (18K to 24K) of your gold ornaments, excluding gems/stones, calculated at the prevailing RBI-approved market rate.

Is my gold safe with the lender?

Yes, 100%. Partner banks and NBFCs store your pledged gold in certified tamper-evident packaging inside vault lockers protected by 24/7 armed security and comprehensive insurance.

Can I repay before the tenure ends?

Yes, you can prepay or foreclose your gold loan at any time. Most floating-rate gold loans carry zero or negligible prepayment charges.

What happens to my gold once the loan is repaid?

Upon clearance of the total outstanding dues, your intact, sealed gold packets are immediately returned to you in the exact physical condition in which they were pledged.
