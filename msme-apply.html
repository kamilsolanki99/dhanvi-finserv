MSME / Udyam & Gumasta

Apply for MSME assistance

Complete this secure multi-step application for a new Udyam registration, an Udyam update, a Gumasta / Shop & Establishment licence or business registration assistance. Your progress can be saved and resumed, and documents are uploaded through a private link after submission. Submitting this form starts an assistance request - it does not register your enterprise or issue a certificate with any government portal.

Step 1 of 7

Applicant & address

Enter the applicant or authorised person details exactly as they appear on the PAN and business records. We store only a masked Aadhaar reference and never ask for an Aadhaar OTP on this page.

Aadhaar authentication is used for Udyam registration where applicable. We never store your full Aadhaar number.

Step 2 of 7

Business profile

Tell us about the legal entity, its constitution and the place where it operates. This determines the documents and the registration route.

Business / establishment address

For Gumasta, the establishment address must match the premises proof you upload. For Udyam, use the principal place of business.

Step 3 of 7

Enterprise classification

These figures determine the Micro, Small or Medium classification used for Udyam and several bank schemes. Enter your best estimate; we reconcile it with your records before applying.

Indicative enterprise category

Enter the investment, turnover and employee count to see an indicative classification. The final Micro, Small or Medium category is determined by the Udyam portal on the basis of the prescribed criteria and your verified records.

Investment means the investment in plant and machinery or equipment, excluding land and building. Turnover means the total turnover declared for the relevant year. Do not include personal assets.

Step 4 of 7

Service requirement

Select the registration service you need. The fields below change based on your selection.

Udyam update details

Gumasta / Shop & Establishment details

Gumasta is a state / municipal Shop & Establishment registration. The exact form, fee and validity depend on the establishment type, premises and local municipal rules.

Business registration assistance

Step 5 of 7

Proprietor, partner & director details

Add every proprietor, partner, designated partner or director. The first row is treated as the primary promoter. These details must match the PAN and incorporation documents.

You can add up to 10 people. Aadhaar is optional and is stored only as a masked reference. Do not enter a DIN unless the person is a director.

Step 6 of 7

Document checklist

This list is based on your answers. After submitting, you will receive a private upload link. Upload clear PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP files (maximum 5 MB each).

Documents we will need

Keep these ready

PAN and Aadhaar, a photograph of the proprietor / partners / directors, address proof of the applicant and the business premises, rent agreement or NOC where the premises are rented, business registration proof, existing Udyam or Shop & Establishment certificate where applicable, electricity bill or property tax receipt and any supporting documents. We confirm the final list after reviewing your constitution and service type.

Step 7 of 7

Review & consent

Check your details before submitting. This creates an MSME assistance request; it does not register your enterprise, update Udyam, issue a Gumasta licence or complete any government registration. Those actions happen only after document verification, your approval and actual action on the relevant government portal.

Dhanvi Finserv is an assistance service, not the Udyam portal, the GST Department or a municipal corporation. Registration approval, certificate issuance, fees and penalties are controlled by the relevant government authorities. Never share portal passwords, OTPs or DSC PINs over WhatsApp or email.

Step 1 of 7

MSME assistance request received

Thank you. Our MSME team will review your application and contact you using the details you provided. This is not a completed Udyam, Gumasta or business registration and no government certificate has been issued yet.

Application reference —

Keep this reference safe. Upload your PAN, Aadhaar, photograph, address proof, business proof, rent agreement or NOC, existing Udyam or Shop & Establishment certificate and supporting documents securely against this application.
